# Prose Review: ArchShell PRD Addendum

**Reviewed:** `addendum.md` (current working tree, after the structure findings were applied)
**Reader type:** humans. **Style guide:** Microsoft Writing Style Guide.
**Prior pass:** structure findings in `review-structure-addendum.md` are already applied; none are re-raised here.

**Purpose read:** This document exists to help downstream architects find the technical rationale behind the PRD: where the design lives, which mechanisms were chosen or rejected for FR-43–FR-60, and the market context for positioning.

**Style notes to preserve:** terse reference register; sentence fragments as section openers ("Covers FR-…", "Chosen: …", "Rejected alternative(s): …"); FR/README cross-reference shorthand (§, en-dash ranges); "(inference)" labels and the research caveat; technical jargon (building blocks, intents, charter growth, pack). The fixes below keep that voice and change only what impedes comprehension.
